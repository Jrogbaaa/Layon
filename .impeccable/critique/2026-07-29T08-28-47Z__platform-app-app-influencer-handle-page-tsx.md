## Anti-Patterns Verdict

The page mostly avoids generic AI output because Medianoche is distinctive and disciplined.
The weaker composition shows dashboard-template reflexes: a six-item metric band, seven
identical daily cards, repeated slug sections, and an always-expanded analytics conveyor.
The deterministic detector returned zero findings for the target. Browser evidence was not
available, so no live overlay was produced.

## Cognitive Load and Emotional Journey

Cognitive load is high for fast triage: seven of eight checks fail. The page has a strong,
premium arrival and early analytical peak, then enters a long mid-page valley before the
recommendation. Trust context is weakest at the high-stakes recommendation moment, and the
page ends on passive signals instead of a decision.

## Strengths

- Distinctive garnet/amber visual system with disciplined typographic roles.
- Noise-gated deltas, sample counts, semantic structures, and exact values support trust.
- Strong accessibility foundations in chart controls, focus, reduced motion, and tables.

## Priority Issues

### P1 — No decision-first triage spine

Put freshness, meaningful status, and current strategy/action near the hero so the manager
does not cross four analytical regions before learning what matters.

### P1 — Missing trust context at the decision point

Show capture freshness and strategy/recommendation currency beside the shared strategy and
brief, not in a distant footer.

### P1 — Planned additions risk worsening overload

Use inline progressive disclosure for editing and preserve one clear primary action instead
of adding another always-expanded full-width dashboard block.

### P2 — Repeated cards weaken scan speed

Keep the existing analysis intact for this scoped feature, but avoid reproducing the repeated
card vocabulary in new strategy/freshness UI.

### P2 — Fragile microtype and touch targets

Use at least 12px for essential explanation and familiar, comfortably sized controls.

## Persona Red Flags

- Impatient managers cannot reach the decision without scrolling through the report.
- Accessibility-dependent users face essential 10px explanation and small compact links.
- Talent managers cannot tell whether evidence is fresh enough to repeat in a meeting.

## Minor Observations

- Greatest Hits overstates completeness unless lifetime history is truly available.
- Engagement rate explains the denominator but not the post window.
- Daily delta cards do not consistently apply the headline Noise Rule.
- Some links remain Spanish in English mode.
- Signals lack timestamp/evidence-strength context.

## Provocative Questions

- If a manager reads one sentence before calling talent, what should it say?

- Which evidence is needed daily versus only during investigation?
- Should the page end on an observation or a committed next action?
